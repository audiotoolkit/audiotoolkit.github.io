How It Works

How to Extract Audio from a Video

1. Upload your video: Drag and drop an MP4, MOV, or WEBM file into the upload area, or click to browse your device.

2. Choose your output format: Pick WAV for lossless quality, or MP3 for a smaller file that plays everywhere — with a bitrate to match.

3. Extract and download: Click the button and just the audio track saves straight to your device as a new file. Your original video is untouched.

Why It Matters

Why Extract Audio Locally in Your Browser?

Most free video-to-MP3 sites upload your entire video file to a server, strip the audio there, then send back a download link — meaning a video that might be private, unreleased, or simply large sits on someone else's server in the meantime. This tool decodes your video's audio track using your browser's own Web Audio API, so the video file never leaves your device at all.

It's also usually much faster for this exact reason: there's no multi-minute upload of a large video file over the network before conversion can even start.

Good To Know

Video Formats and Limitations

MP4 and MOV with standard H.264 video and AAC audio work in essentially every modern browser. WEBM usually works too. Less common codecs inside these containers can occasionally fail to decode — this is a browser limitation, not something specific to this tool.

If a file fails to convert, try Chrome or Edge, which tend to have the broadest built-in codec support, or re-export the video as a standard MP4 first using any video editor.

A note on the MP3 encoder: MP3 output relies on an encoder library loaded from a public CDN. If your network blocks it or it fails to load, this tool will disable the MP3 option and let you use WAV instead, rather than producing a broken file.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video file uploaded to a server? ▾
No. Your video is decoded locally in your browser using the Web Audio API, and only the audio track is extracted and encoded. The video file is never uploaded anywhere.
What video formats can I extract audio from? ▾
MP4 and MOV files with AAC audio work in most browsers, and WEBM usually works too. Support depends on your browser's built-in decoder, so an unusual codec may occasionally fail to decode.
Does this work for MP4 to MP3? ▾
Yes. MP4 is the most common input for this tool, and the output can be MP3 or WAV, at your choice of bitrate for MP3.
Will this affect the quality of my video? ▾
This tool only reads your video to extract its audio track — it never modifies or re-saves the original video file.
Why did my file fail to convert? ▾
This usually means the browser could not decode that specific video codec. Try a different browser (Chrome generally has the broadest support), or re-export the video as a standard MP4 (H.264/AAC) first.
