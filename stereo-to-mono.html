Why It Matters

Why Convert to Mono?

Voice recordings, podcasts, and interviews are usually captured or processed as stereo by default, even though there's rarely any meaningful left/right separation in the content. Converting to mono cuts the file size roughly in half and avoids inconsistent volume between ears when the original left/right channels weren't recorded evenly.

Some older phone systems, IVR platforms, and hardware devices also specifically require mono audio and will reject or distort stereo files.

Good To Know

How the Channels Are Combined

This tool averages the left and right channel at every point in the audio, which is the standard way to fold stereo down to mono without losing volume or introducing artifacts. If your file has music with strong stereo panning (instruments hard-left or hard-right), the content is preserved, but the stereo width itself is naturally lost — that's an inherent trade-off of mono, not a limitation of this tool specifically.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert stereo audio to mono? ▾
Common reasons include: voice recordings and podcasts rarely need stereo separation, mono files are roughly half the size of stereo, some phone systems and older hardware only accept mono audio, and mono avoids the left/right imbalance that can happen with poorly recorded interviews.
How does this tool combine the channels?
It averages the left and right channels together at every point in the audio, which preserves the overall sound while merging them into one channel. This is the standard approach used by most professional audio software.
Will converting to mono lose any audio?
If a sound was panned entirely to one side (for example, a stereo music mix with instruments hard-panned left or right), averaging will still include it, just without the left/right positioning. True mono has no stereo width, so any intentional stereo effects are lost.
Is my audio file uploaded to a server?
No. The channel conversion happens locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your file is never uploaded.
