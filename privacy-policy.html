Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Audio Toolkit provides free browser-based audio tools. This page explains what happens to your files and what, if any, information we collect.

Your Audio Files

Every tool on this site processes your audio file locally, inside your own browser, using the Web Audio API. Your file is never uploaded to our servers or to any third party — there is no upload step at all. When you close the tab, the file and any trimmed or edited output are gone unless you've downloaded them.

What We Don't Collect

This site does not use cookies, does not run analytics scripts, and does not load fonts, ads, or tracking scripts from third-party services. There is currently no account system, so we don't collect names, emails, or any personal information through the tools themselves.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as our hosting provider, may log standard server access information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operational purposes, independent of anything we do. You can read GitHub's own privacy statement for details on what they collect as a host.

If This Changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising, or other third-party services to this site, we will update this policy to accurately reflect what is collected and by whom, before those services go live.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our Contact page.
