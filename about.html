About Us

Audio tools that respect your files

Audio Toolkit is a small set of free, browser-based tools for trimming, converting, and editing audio — built so your files never have to leave your device.

Why We Built This

Most free online audio tools work the same way: you upload your file to a server, wait, and download the result. That means every voice memo, unreleased track, or private recording passes through someone else's system along the way.

Audio Toolkit takes a different approach. Our file-based tools run in your browser using the Web Audio API, so your audio file is decoded, edited, and exported without being sent to a server. The exception is Voice to Text, which uses your browser's speech-recognition service and says so on its own page.

What You Can Expect

Processed Locally

Audio files you open in the file tools are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers.

No Signup

No account, no email, no login. Open a tool and start using it right away.

Works on Every Device

Whether you're on a PC, Mac, Android phone, or iPhone, each tool works the same way in any modern browser.

Genuinely Free

Every tool is free to use, with no artificial limits designed to push you toward a paid plan.

Our Tools

We're building out a focused set of audio tools, one at a time, each built to work well rather than rushed out. Right now that includes:

Audio Trimmer — cut any audio file down to exactly the part you need, with a waveform preview and instant download.

MP3 Converter — convert audio between formats, entirely in your browser.

Video to MP3 — extract audio from MP4, MOV, and WEBM video files.

M4A to MP3 — convert iPhone voice memos and AAC audio to universal MP3.

Audio Merger — combine multiple audio clips into one file, in any order.

Volume Booster — boost or normalize audio volume with clipping protection.

WAV to MP3, MP3 to WAV, OGG to MP3, MP3 to OGG, and FLAC to MP3 — dedicated format converters.

MP4 to WAV and M4A to WAV — lossless conversions.

Audio Reverser — play any audio file backwards.

Fade In/Out Adder — smooth out abrupt starts and endings.

Silence Remover, Speed Changer, and Voice Recorder.

Voice to Text — live speech dictation.

AAC to MP3, Ringtone Maker, and MP3 Tag Editor.

Stereo to Mono, Audio Loop Maker, and Waveform Image Generator.

Metronome, Guitar Tuner, and BPM Detector.

Audio Equalizer, Text to Speech, and Vocal Remover.

Audio Splitter, Sample Rate Converter, and White Noise Generator.

More tools are actively being added.
