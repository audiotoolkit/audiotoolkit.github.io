How It Works

How to Find a Song's BPM

1. Upload your track. Drag and drop a file into the upload area, or click to browse your device.

2. Get an automatic estimate. The tool analyzes the beat pattern and shows a BPM guess within a couple of seconds.

3. Adjust if needed. If it looks like exactly half or double the real tempo, use the ÷2 / ×2 buttons. For tricky tracks, tap along manually instead.

Why It Matters

Being Honest About Accuracy

Automatic BPM detection works by finding energy peaks in the audio (likely beats) and measuring the most common interval between them. This works well for music with a clear, steady, prominent beat — most electronic, pop, hip-hop, and rock tracks. It's less reliable for music with sparse percussion, live/acoustic performances with natural tempo variation, or complex rhythms with multiple competing pulses.

Rather than present an automatic guess as infallible, this tool includes a manual Tap Tempo option specifically because automatic detection has real limits — for a track where the estimate looks wrong, tapping along yourself is the more reliable method.

Good To Know

The Half/Double Tempo Issue

The single most common BPM detection mistake, across every tool that does this — not just this one — is locking onto a secondary beat instead of the main pulse, producing exactly half or double the real tempo. If a detected BPM feels obviously too fast or too slow for the song's actual feel, try the ÷2 or ×2 button before assuming it's simply wrong.
