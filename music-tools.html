Music Tools Online

Free online music tools for ringtones, BPM detection, guitar tuning, looping, tag editing, vocal reduction, and waveform creation.

These tools cover practical music tasks from preparing a ringtone to checking tempo, editing metadata, creating loops, and working with vocals. Use the dedicated tool for each job and check its supported formats before processing a file.

Choose a tool

Ringtone Maker – Create a Ringtone Online Free

Create a ringtone from any song or audio file, free and online. Pick the exact clip, preview it, and download — entirely in your browser.

BPM Detector – Find a Song's Tempo Online Free

Estimate the BPM (tempo) of any audio file online, free. Upload a track and get an instant estimate — entirely in your browser.

Guitar Tuner – Free Online, Uses Your Microphone

Tune your guitar online, free, using your device's microphone. Real-time pitch detection, works for any instrument — entirely in your browser.

Audio Loop Maker – Repeat a Clip Online Free

Repeat any audio clip to make a loop, free and online. Set the number of repeats or a target length — entirely in your browser.

MP3 Tag Editor Online – Edit ID3 Metadata Free

Edit MP3 metadata such as title, artist, album, genre, and track information online. Make your music files easier to organize.

Vocal Remover – Free Karaoke Track Maker Online

Remove or reduce vocals from a song online, free, for a rough karaoke track. Honest about its limits — entirely in your browser, no upload.

Waveform Image Generator – Audio to PNG Free

Turn any audio file into a downloadable waveform image, free and online. Customize colors and style — entirely in your browser, no upload.

Audio Equalizer – Adjust Bass, Mid, Treble Free

Adjust the bass, mid, and treble of any audio file online, free. Preview changes live, then download — entirely in your browser.

Fade In/Out Adder – Smooth Audio Transitions Free

Add a fade in or fade out to any audio file online, free. Smooth, click-free starts and endings — preview instantly, entirely in your browser.

Audio Speed Changer – Speed Up or Slow Down Free

Speed up or slow down any audio file online, free. Adjust from 0.5x to 2x, preview instantly, and download — entirely in your browser.

Why use a dedicated audio tool?

Focused tools can be faster to understand than a full audio editor when you only need one operation. Choose the tool that matches your task, check the supported input and output formats, and preview your result when the tool provides a preview.

Privacy and file handling

Audio Toolkit is a collection of browser-based tools, but privacy behavior can differ by feature. Read the privacy note on the individual tool page before processing sensitive recordings. In particular, browser speech recognition may involve the browser or its speech service.
