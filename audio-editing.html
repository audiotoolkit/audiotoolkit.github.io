Audio Editing Tools Online

Edit audio online with free tools for trimming, splitting, merging, fading, reversing, changing speed, removing silence, boosting volume, and compressing files.

Use these browser-based tools for common audio cleanup and editing jobs. Each tool focuses on one task so you can make a targeted change without learning a full desktop audio editor.

Choose a tool

Trim Audio Online Free – Cut MP3, WAV & More

Trim MP3, WAV, M4A and other audio files online for free. Select the exact start and end, preview the result, and download the trimmed audio.

Audio Splitter – Split One File Into Many Free

Split an audio file into equal parts or at custom points, free and online. Download each piece separately — entirely in your browser.

Audio Merger – Combine Multiple Audio Files Free

Merge or join multiple audio files into one, free and online. Reorder clips, preview, and download — entirely in your browser, no upload.

Fade In/Out Adder – Smooth Audio Transitions Free

Add a fade in or fade out to any audio file online, free. Smooth, click-free starts and endings — preview instantly, entirely in your browser.

Audio Reverser – Reverse Any Audio File Free

Reverse any audio file online, free. Play it backwards, preview instantly, and download — entirely in your browser, no upload.

Audio Speed Changer – Speed Up or Slow Down Free

Speed up or slow down any audio file online, free. Adjust from 0.5x to 2x, preview instantly, and download — entirely in your browser.

Silence Remover Online – Remove Dead Air Free

Remove leading and trailing silence from audio online for free. Clean recordings, voice notes, and clips without manually trimming every file.

Volume Booster Online – Increase Audio Volume Free

Increase or normalize the volume of an audio file online for free. Preview the result and download louder audio while reducing the risk of clipping.

Audio Compressor – Reduce File Size Free

Shrink any audio file's size online, free. Pick a bitrate or target size, preview the estimate, and download — entirely in your browser.

Audio Equalizer – Adjust Bass, Mid, Treble Free

Adjust the bass, mid, and treble of any audio file online, free. Preview changes live, then download — entirely in your browser.

Sample Rate Converter Online – Convert Audio Free

Convert audio sample rates online for free, including common rates such as 44.1 kHz and 48 kHz. Learn when changing sample rate is useful.

Stereo to Mono Converter – Free Online

Convert stereo audio to mono online, free. Combine left and right channels into one, reduce file size, fix phone compatibility — entirely in your browser.

Why use a dedicated audio tool?

Focused tools can be faster to understand than a full audio editor when you only need one operation. Choose the tool that matches your task, check the supported input and output formats, and preview your result when the tool provides a preview.

Privacy and file handling

Audio Toolkit is a collection of browser-based tools, but privacy behavior can differ by feature. Read the privacy note on the individual tool page before processing sensitive recordings. In particular, browser speech recognition may involve the browser or its speech service.
