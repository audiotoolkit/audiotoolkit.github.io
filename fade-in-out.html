How It Works

How to Add a Fade In or Fade Out

1. Upload your audio. Drag and drop a file into the upload area, or click to browse your device.

2. Set your fade durations. Drag the Fade In and Fade Out sliders independently — leave either at 0 to skip it.

3. Preview and download. Listen to the result, then download it as a WAV file once it sounds right.

Why It Matters

Why Add a Fade?

When audio starts or stops at a point where the waveform isn't near zero, it creates an audible click or pop — especially noticeable with trimmed clips, loops, or clips that will be placed next to other audio. A short fade eases the volume in or out instead of cutting sharply.

This tool calculates and applies a smooth linear fade directly to the waveform in your browser, so there's no upload wait and no quality loss from re-encoding.

Good To Know

Picking Fade Durations

To just remove a click at a hard cut: 10\u201350 milliseconds (0.01\u20130.05 on the slider) is usually enough and won't be noticeable as a "fade" at all.

For a musical fade-out ending: 2\u20135 seconds is typical, giving the track a natural, gradual close.

Combine with trimming: if you're cutting a clip down first, use the Audio Trimmer, then add a fade here to clean up the new start or end point.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does audio need a fade in or fade out? ▾
Audio that starts or stops abruptly, especially mid-waveform, can produce an audible click or pop. A short fade smooths that transition, which is standard practice for music, podcasts, and any clip that will be looped or placed next to other audio.
How long should a fade be?
For removing a click at a hard cut, 10–50 milliseconds is often enough and barely noticeable. For a musical fade-out ending, 2–5 seconds is more typical. This tool lets you set any duration up to 10 seconds.
Can I add both a fade in and fade out at once?
Yes. Set a duration for either or both independently — leave one at zero to skip it.
Is my audio file uploaded to a server?
No. The fade is calculated and applied locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your file is never uploaded.
