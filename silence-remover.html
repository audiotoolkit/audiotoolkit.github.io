How It Works

How to Remove Silence from Audio

1. Upload your audio. Drag and drop a file into the upload area, or click to browse your device.

2. Adjust sensitivity if needed. The default Medium setting works for most recordings. Increase it for noisier recordings, decrease it if quiet parts are being cut.

3. Preview and download. Listen to the trimmed result, then download it as a WAV file.

Why It Matters

What This Tool Actually Trims

This tool removes silence from the very start and very end of a recording — the dead air before someone starts speaking, or after a recording accidentally kept running. It deliberately does not remove pauses in the middle of your audio, since those are usually intentional (breath pauses, gaps between sentences, musical rests) and removing them would make speech sound unnaturally rushed.

Detection and trimming happen locally in your browser using the Web Audio API, so your recording is never uploaded anywhere.

Good To Know

Choosing the Right Sensitivity

Low sensitivity only trims near-total silence, which is safer for quiet recordings but may leave a little dead air behind.

High sensitivity trims more aggressively, including faint background noise, but risks cutting into a very quiet start of speech if pushed too far.

If a recording gets cut short: lower the sensitivity and try again — the preview lets you check before downloading.
