How It Works

How to Use the Metronome

1. Set your tempo. Drag the slider, use the +/- buttons, or tap the "Tap Tempo" button in rhythm to set the BPM automatically.

2. Pick a time signature. The first beat of each measure gets a distinct accented click.

3. Press Start. The metronome clicks steadily until you press Stop.

Why It Matters

Why the Timing Is Precise

Simple metronome implementations often use JavaScript's setInterval, which is not reliable for precise audio timing — it can drift by tens of milliseconds, especially if the browser tab is busy. This metronome instead uses the Web Audio API's own high-precision clock with a look-ahead scheduler, scheduling clicks slightly ahead of when they're needed so the actual playback stays locked to the beat.

Good To Know

Tips for Practicing with a Metronome

Start slower than your target tempo. Practicing accurately at a slower speed builds the timing habit better than fighting through mistakes at full speed.

Use the accented downbeat to keep track of where you are in the measure, especially useful for time signatures like 3/4 or 6/8.

Keep the tab open and active. Browsers can throttle timers in inactive background tabs, which may affect timing if you switch away.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this metronome's timing? ▾
It uses the Web Audio API's own audio clock with a look-ahead scheduler, the same technique used by professional web-based audio tools, rather than JavaScript's regular timers which can drift. This keeps the click precisely on time even during long practice sessions.
What is tap tempo?
Tap tempo lets you set the BPM by tapping a button in rhythm with a song or your own playing, instead of typing in a number. Tap at least four times for an accurate reading.
Can I change the time signature?
Yes. Choose from common time signatures, and the first beat of each measure is accented with a distinct click so you can hear the downbeat.
Do I need to download anything?
No. This metronome runs entirely in your browser tab — just open the page and press start.
