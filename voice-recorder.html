How It Works

How to Record Your Voice Online

1. Click "Start Recording." Your browser will ask permission to use your microphone the first time — allow it to continue.

2. Speak, then click "Stop." A timer shows how long you've been recording.

3. Listen back. Play your recording right on the page before deciding to keep it.

4. Download. Save it as a WAV file, ready to use, edit, or share anywhere.

Why It Matters

Your Recording Stays on Your Device

Recording audio in a browser normally requires explicit microphone permission — a security feature built into every modern browser, not something specific to this site. Once you grant it, the actual audio data captured by this tool is processed and stored only in your browser's memory. It is never streamed, uploaded, or sent to any server, at any point, even while you're recording.

Good To Know

Tips for a Clean Recording

Use headphones with a mic if you have them. Built-in laptop microphones pick up more room echo and background noise than a headset mic.

Find a quiet spot. This tool doesn't apply any noise reduction, so background noise will be captured as-is.

Leave a beat of silence at the start and end. It gives you room to trim cleanly afterward with the Audio Trimmer or Silence Remover.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my recording sent anywhere while I record? ▾
No. Recording uses your browser's built-in microphone access and stays entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded, streamed, or sent anywhere unless you choose to share the downloaded file yourself.
Why does my browser ask for microphone permission?
This is a standard browser security requirement for any site that records audio — it ensures no website can access your microphone without your explicit consent. You can deny it or revoke it later in your browser's site settings.
What format is the recording saved in?
You can download it as a WAV file (universal, lossless), or in your browser's native recording format if you prefer the smaller original file.
Can I re-record if I make a mistake?
Yes. Just click Record again — the previous take is discarded once you start a new one, so download anything you want to keep first.
