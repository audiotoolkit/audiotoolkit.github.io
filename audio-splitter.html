How It Works

How to Split an Audio File

1. Upload your audio. Drag and drop a file into the upload area, or click to browse your device.

2. Choose Equal Parts or Custom Points. Equal Parts divides the file evenly by count; Custom Points lets you enter exact timestamps to split at.

3. Click Split File. Each resulting part appears in a list with its own download button.

4. Download each part. Click the download button next to each part you want to keep.

Why It Matters

Common Uses for Splitting Audio

Splitting a long recording into separate files is useful for turning a single long podcast recording into individual episodes, breaking an audiobook chapter recording into smaller tracks, or dividing a long lecture into study-sized segments. This tool copies the underlying audio samples directly without re-encoding, so every resulting part keeps full original quality.

Good To Know

Choosing Split Points

Equal Parts is the fastest option when you just need a long file broken into roughly even chunks, regardless of exact content boundaries.

Custom Points gives you control when the natural breaks in your audio aren't evenly spaced — for example, splitting a recorded meeting at each topic change. Enter timestamps as seconds (e.g. "45, 180, 320").

Find exact split points first by previewing your file elsewhere, or use the Waveform Image Generator to visually spot pauses between sections.
