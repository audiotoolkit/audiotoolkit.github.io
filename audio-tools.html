Choose the right audio tool

Audio Toolkit groups common audio tasks into focused browser-based tools. Start with a category when you know what you want to do, or open a specific converter or editor when you already know the format or task.

For format changes, use Audio Converters for MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC and related workflows. For editing, use tools for trimming, splitting, merging, fading, reversing, changing speed, boosting volume and removing silence. Voice and Speech tools cover recording, speech-to-text and text-to-speech, while Music Tools cover ringtones, BPM, guitar tuning, loops, tags and vocal workflows.

Most processing is designed to happen in your browser, so your files do not need to be sent through a server upload queue. Browser support and available processing power can vary by device and file, so keep your original file until you have checked the downloaded result.

Common tasks and where to start

  • Shorten a recording or remove an intro: Audio Trimmer, then Fade In/Out Adder for a smooth start and end.
  • Make an iPhone voice memo work everywhere: M4A to MP3 Converter.
  • Join several clips into one file: Audio Merger.
  • Make quiet audio louder: Volume Booster.
  • Find a song's tempo: BPM Detector; practice with the Metronome.
  • Record your voice or dictate text: Voice Recorder and Voice to Text.

File-based tools process audio in your browser. Voice to Text is the exception, because browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser vendor. Each tool page explains what applies.
